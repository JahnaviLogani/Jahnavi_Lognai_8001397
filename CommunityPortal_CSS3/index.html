Description

The Local Community Event Portal provides residents with information about events, workshops, awareness programs, community services, and public engagement activities. The portal is designed to improve communication between the city council and citizens while promoting participation in local initiatives.

Community Bulletin

The city council is organizing multiple awareness campaigns, cultural celebrations, educational workshops, and volunteer programs throughout the year. Residents are encouraged to participate in local development initiatives and contribute to creating a stronger community. Registration for upcoming events can be completed online. Additional services including public assistance programs, environmental campaigns, and health awareness activities are available through the community portal.
